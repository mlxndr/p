## Negative Politeness

@pause

@table caps
| politeness strategy | linguistic form |
|---|---|
| *Don’t presume* | Question |
| *Give hearer options* | Be pessimistic |
| *Minimize FTA* | Make explicit rate of imposition |
| *Redress other wants* | Go on record as owing or forgiving a debt |
| *Communicate desire not to impose* | Apologise |
| *Disassociate from imposition* | Avoid first-person pronouns, state as general rule |

## Positive Politeness

@pause

@table caps
| politeness strategy | linguistic form |
|---|---|
| *Claim common ground* | Attend to hearer’s interests, seek agreement, joke, state common ground |
| *Show admiration/interest* | Exaggerate interest/approval/sympathy for hearer |
| *Convey cooperation* | Show concern for hearer’s wants, give reasons |
| *Claim reflexivity* | Be optimistic, include the hearer in the activity |
| *Fulfil one of hearer’s desires* | Give something to hearer (eg things, sympathy, understanding, cooperation) |
