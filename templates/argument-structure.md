## Toulmin Model (Alternative Format)

**Claim:** What you're arguing

**Data:** Evidence supporting the claim

**Warrant:** Why the data supports the claim

**Backing:** Additional support for the warrant

**Qualifier:** Degree of certainty (possibly, probably, certainly)

**Rebuttal:** Conditions under which the claim might not hold
